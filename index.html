In the Shallow Reef

The Jorunna Parva, also know as the seabunny, are apart of the nudibranch. They resemble that of a shelless mollusc (or slug,) while thier reniophores (atenna) resemble the shape of bunny ears, and lastly thier papillae throughout thier body making them look furry. The seabunny has eyes, but thier eyesight is only limited to light and shadows, the papillae act as sensors to help them navigate thier surrondings. These creatures are small in size, only coming to about 2.5cm long.

This species is known to inhabit areas of; Japan, Papua New Guinea, Tanzania, Philippines,and Seychelles. But despite this it is believed they could be found anywhere as long as it has an abundance of food. Thier diet consist of things like algea, seagrass, and even sea slugs; but mainly, thier diet consist of toxic sea sponges which they in turn proccess and create a natural poison for defense
